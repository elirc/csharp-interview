**Output:** offers. Everything here converts the skills from Phases 1–2 into interview performance across the four practical formats.

Applications went out end of week 6/7. From week 8, real interviews and prep interleave — **real interviews are the curriculum now**; every one ends with the same-day retro prompt (in [../00-setup/02-ai-workflow.md](../00-setup/02-ai-workflow.md)).

## Weekly shape (weeks 8–10)

| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| Mon | Question-bank drill + one system-design mock · vault: `L` + `A` |
| Tue | Live-coding mock (45 min) + retro · vault: `W` + `E` |
| Wed | Take-home block (wk 8/9) or Bookable polish from interview feedback · vault: `S` + `X` |
| Thu | Question-bank drill + behavioral rehearsal · vault: `T` + `D`/`H`/`O` (rotate) |
| Fri | Debugging drill (interview mode: 30 min, narrated) + SQL maintenance set + [gotcha gauntlet](../04-question-vault/12-code-output-gotchas.md) |
| Sat | Rebuild drill targeting whatever this week's mocks exposed |
| Sun | Full mock loop once per week: 15 min behavioral + 45 min live coding + 30 min design, single sitting — stamina is trainable. From week 7, use a [mock screener script](../04-question-vault/14-mock-screener-scripts.md) |

Plus 10 minutes of [rapid-fire trivia](../04-question-vault/11-rapid-fire-trivia.md) daily, out loud. It's the cheapest habit in the curriculum and it's what keeps the trivia layer from decaying between mocks.

## Priorities if time-crunched (e.g., interviews arrive early)

1. **The Bookable deep-dive story** — you control this material; most interviews start with "tell me about a project." Highest ROI per hour.
2. **Live-coding reps** — most likely single point of failure.
3. **Question bank ⭐-marked items** — the 18 highest-frequency questions.
4. **SQL maintenance** — one top-N-per-group per week minimum.
5. Everything else.

## After each real interview (non-negotiable ritual)

Same day, 30 minutes: run the interview-retro prompt with everything you remember — questions asked, your answers, where you froze. Weak spots feed Saturday's rebuild and the week's drills. Three real interviews with honest retros are worth more than three weeks of solo prep — treat early rejections as paid training.
